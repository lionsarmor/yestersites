STATION LOG   /   OCTOBER 16, 1999

The Saturday log

Host Clem Orr takes the overnight shift. Len Rusk has the transmitter keys. All times are Pacific.

Program order

  • 12:00 AMDry lake weather. Clem reads the gauge outside the pump house. The needle has pointed to “fair” since the roof came off.
  • 01:00 AMLost & found frequency. A baker hears a polka in his flour sifter. Len demonstrates what a loose belt sounds like. The baker prefers the polka.
  • 02:13 AMThe motel ice machine. Mae Vale calls in from the Blue Moon. Play the aircheck.
  • 03:00 AMWrong-number hour. Clem calls back the people who asked for the radiator shop. Tonight’s topic is a 1973 station wagon that only overheats on birthdays.

A correction from Friday

The appliance making three descending tones on the faxed report was a bread machine. We owe the entire electric blanket community a correction. Len has amended the station log in pencil.
