CRATER COURT PRESS BOX

The shot that took two days.

By Nell Torque, courtside correspondent

Friday, 9:14 PM. Puck Basalt takes a pass at half court and flicks the ball beyond the floodlights. The horn sounds. Both benches develop neck pain.

Saturday, 11:06 AM. A weather satellite reports an orange object with unusually good rotation. The Comet Tails extend their hotel reservation.

Sunday, 2:51 PM. The ball finally drops through. Groundskeeper Len Whisk finds it in a mop bucket underneath the basket. His signed statement takes up both sides of the receipt.

The ruling

Under paragraph 8 of the league handbook, the game ends when the arena’s parking permit expires. The permit ran out Saturday at noon. Vacuum wins by one.

“I still had a clean look,” Puck said, rubbing the little dent the moon left in his elbow.

FINAL • GAME 7
Vacuum Cleaners 88
Comet Tails 87
Crater Court · ruling upheld
SCORER’S NOTE

Sunday’s basket has been recorded as the opening two points of next season, pending a home game on the same moon.
