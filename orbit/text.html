Moon Hoops '99

Inter-Lunar Basketball Association, 1999 Finals Edition.

Final

Vacuum Cleaners 88, Comet Tails 87. Puck Basalt's final shot left lunar airspace on Friday and came down on Sunday. Officials ruled the parking permit expired Saturday, ending the game before the basket.

The players

  • Vee Sump, Vacuum Cleaners. A suction-forward out of Mare Imbrium. Vee once pulled down a rebound, both shoelaces, and a loose filling from seat 8C. Only the rebound counted.
  • Puck Basalt, Comet Tails. Quarried on a Tuesday. Joined the league on a Thursday. Puck shoots with enough backspin to bring the ball home eventually.
  • Gee Whillik, Europa Slip. Four eyes, zero peripheral vision. Gee makes every pass by reading the reflection in the opposing coach’s visor.

The arenas

  • Crater Court, The Moon. Section D hangs over a crater. The cheap seats get an excellent view of the expensive seats falling in. All jump balls must be tethered to a line judge.
  • Europa Gardens, Europa. Players enter through the freezer aisle. The local crowd throws mittens after a hat trick, regardless of the sport. The last player sliding gets to inbound.
  • The Tailgate, Comet 24B. The stadium moves. Your ticket lists coordinates and the exact second your seat will reach them. Away teams may call one timeout to catch up.

Practice possession

You're down one and Len the groundskeeper is parked under the rim. Choose a play.

  1. Bank it.
  2. Loft it.
  3. Pass to Len.

Bank. The crooked backboard sends it straight in. Two points.

Lob. Your ball enters orbit. The horn sounds.

Pass. Len tips it in with his buffer handle. His signed work order qualifies him as a substitute.
