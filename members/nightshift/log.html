October signal reports

Oct 16 · P. Bell, Cold Storage Road. “You came in clear as anything in the walk-in freezer. Stepped into the kitchen and lost you completely.” Len thinks the freezer door is acting as a reflector. Clem would like a report from the dairy aisle.

Oct 15 · T. Gray, Route 8. “Reception dropped every time I passed the billboard with the giant soup can.” The engineer has entered this into the obstructions register under vegetables.

Oct 14 · M. Vale, Blue Moon Motor Lodge. “Signal fine. Your clock runs four minutes slow.” Mae runs the night desk at the Blue Moon. She sets her watch by a truck that turns up at 4:17 every morning, including Sundays.
