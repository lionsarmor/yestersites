The sticker shelf

Homemade loops for your own little corner of the web. Each sticker fits in a 64 × 56 pixel box.

Puddleplum

I drew whiskers on a purple cat and June asked why my eggplant had radio aerials. I gave him a coat. He has since become unbearable about the weather.

The leaf collar takes three greens. The boots are the same two brown pixels flipped over.

Diskette Dan

Dan keeps his shopping list on himself. To read it, he has to climb into the drive. By the time he gets out he has forgotten the eggs.

I moved each shoe down a pixel on alternate frames. The white label stays level so he looks busy.

The Bread Post

Put in one slice and a letter pops up for someone you haven’t seen since grade school. It has sent Carol seven slices and no explanation.

The toast moves eight pixels. The toaster stays still. I took the crumbs out of frame three because they looked like smoke.
