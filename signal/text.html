The Committee for the Extra Moon

Mulberry Lane Astronomical Circular, No. 7, October 1997.

Our neighbor has a moon.

At 11:42 on Tuesday, the Hendersons’ garage acquired a satellite. I got one exposure through the kitchen window before Mr. Henderson took the bins out. At 11:43, the sky went back to its usual arrangement.

Plate A shows three bright spots. One is a kitchen reflection, the high one is the Moon, and the low one appears to be on a stalk above the garage. The treasurer produced a hardware receipt. Three trials of the neighbor's light switch made the lower object disappear. The committee has adjourned until Thursday.
