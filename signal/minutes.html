Minutes of the emergency sitting

CARBON COPY • SECRETARY’S FILE / 07

Tuesday, 14 October

Present at the card table: Mabel Vellum, June Quill, and Treasurer Len Crumb. Guest: a cardigan belonging to the Hendersons, mistaken for a fourth member in poor light.

11:51 pm · A financial objection

Len presented a hardware-store receipt for a 60-watt globe. Mabel observed that the document described a globe, which was precisely the committee’s position.

12:06 am · The switch experiment

June stood at the fence. Mr. Henderson operated the switch indoors. Three trials produced three disappearances. On the fourth trial he opened the garage door. No one had ruled a column for that.

12:19 am · Adjournment

The matter carries to Thursday. June will bring a clipboard. Len requests that anyone borrowing the teapot return the little lid.

June Quill, recording secretary


Maintained by June Quill from the dining-room computer.
